# Getting Practical Experience: Attachments and Work-Based Learning

> Start early, apply widely, and treat your attachment as a three-month interview.

## Why it matters
Industrial attachment and other work-based learning (WBL) are often the bridge to your first job. Many graduate roles go to former attachees.

## Finding a placement
1. **Start 3–6 months before** your attachment period. Large organisations open applications early.
2. Use your university's career office and portal, company career pages, LinkedIn and professional networks.
3. Apply to a mix of banks, telcos, start-ups, NGOs, government agencies and software firms. Small companies often give you more hands-on work.
4. Send a tailored CV and short cover letter; follow up politely after a week or two.
5. Track every application: company, role, date, status. TechPath's Opportunities tab can help with the tracking.

## Other ways to gain experience
- Freelance or volunteer projects for local businesses and community groups.
- Hackathons and innovation challenges.
- Open-source contributions.
- Campus roles: lab assistant, tech club lead, IT support.

## Making the most of your attachment
- Agree on goals with your supervisor in week one.
- Keep a weekly log of what you did and learned. It's useful for your report and your CV.
- Ask for code reviews and feedback; volunteer for tasks slightly beyond your comfort zone.
- Be reliable: arrive on time, communicate early when you're stuck, and finish what you start.
- Before you leave, ask for a reference and connect with your team on LinkedIn.

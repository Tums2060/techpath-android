# Behavioural Interviews and the STAR Method

> Prepare five short stories about real situations and tell them with STAR.

## What they're testing
Teamwork, communication, handling pressure, learning from mistakes and motivation. Questions often start with "Tell me about a time when…".

## The STAR method
- **Situation**: the context in one or two sentences.
- **Task**: what you were responsible for.
- **Action**: what *you* did (say "I", not only "we").
- **Result**: what happened, ideally with a number, and what you learned.

## Example
*Tell me about a time you worked under pressure.*
- **S**: Our group's final-year project demo was in three days and the backend kept crashing.
- **T**: I owned the API.
- **A**: I added logging, found a database connection leak, fixed it and wrote a quick load test.
- **R**: The demo ran without errors and we scored an A. I now add logging from day one.

## Stories to prepare
1. A project you're proud of.
2. A conflict or disagreement in a team.
3. A mistake or failure and what you changed.
4. Learning something new quickly.
5. Leading or helping others.

## Questions to ask them
- What does a typical week look like for an intern or junior engineer here?
- How do you review code and give feedback?
- What would success look like in the first three months?

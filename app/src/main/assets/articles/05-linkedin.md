# A LinkedIn Profile for Tech Students

> A clear headline, a short About section and a few visible projects make recruiters' searches find you.

## Essentials
- **Photo**: a clear, friendly headshot with a plain background.
- **Headline**: not just "Student". Try *BICS Student · Android & Backend Developer · Kotlin, Spring Boot, SQL*.
- **About**: 3–5 sentences on what you build, what you're learning and the roles you want.
- **Featured**: pin your best project, a GitHub repo or a portfolio link.
- **Experience and projects**: use the same strong bullets as your CV.
- **Skills**: add 10–15 relevant skills; ask classmates to endorse the important ones.

## Grow your network
- Connect with classmates, lecturers, attachment supervisors and people you meet at events. Add a short note.
- Follow companies you'd like to work for; many post internships on LinkedIn first.
- Join communities such as local developer groups, Google Developer Groups or women-in-tech networks.

## Post occasionally
You don't need to post daily. Share when you finish a project, earn a certificate or attend a hackathon: what you built, what you learned, and a link.

## Turn on "Open to work"
Set it for internships or attachments and choose the locations (for example Nairobi, Remote). You can show it only to recruiters.
